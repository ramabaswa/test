Risk tiers

Tier assigned at intake; it decides approval path, controls, and who can invoke.

Executive summary

  • Five risk tiers from public metadata (auto-approved) to autonomous or regulated actions (ARB, CISO and compliance).
  • PHI access and any write action always require human sign-off and extra controls.
  • A central gateway enforces this at runtime, so approvals are not just paperwork.

Key terms in plain language

  • MCP: a standard way for AI tools to use company systems.
  • Gateway: the single front door that checks who is asking and what they may do.
  • Registry: the approved list of servers and tools.
  • Risk tier: a 0 to 4 rating that decides the approvals needed.
  • PHI: protected health information; it is masked or blocked by default.

Principles

  • Controlled by default, fast by design
  • One front door, many servers
  • Risk-tiered approvals, not one-size-fits-all
  • Reuse the semantic layer; do not copy data into agents
  • Platform team builds the paved road; use case teams drive on it
